                    Inclusion Method

                    NeSy Atlas is a source-backed table of companies whose own homepage, founder, or official announcement explicitly identifies neurosymbolic, neural-symbolic, NeSy, logical-intelligence, or combined neural and symbolic work.

                    Primary homepage evidence: the company's root homepage must contain the case-insensitive term neurosymbolic, neuro-symbolic, neural-symbolic, NeSy, or logical intelligence, or explicitly state that it combines neural and symbolic methods. Company names link directly to the qualifying homepage.

                    For product-first sites that omit architecture language from the homepage, a direct, attributable statement from a founder or official company announcement may qualify the company. Unattributed third-party descriptions, conference sites, and directories do not qualify a company on their own.

                    The shield beside each company opens its specific inclusion evidence, including a short qualifying passage and a link to the relevant homepage section, official company article, research page, or announcement.

                    Current scope: for now, NeSy Atlas focuses on independent and specialist companies and does not include major technology companies, even when they have qualifying neurosymbolic work. This scope may expand in the future.

                    Founded years use each company's LinkedIn About page when available. If a founding year cannot be found there, the current CEO's start year is used as the fallback.
                    Locations and funding reflect the latest public information found during the 04 Aug 2026 review. Funding links point to the relevant disclosure; "Not disclosed" means no reliable public amount was found. GitHub star totals show each verified organization's most-starred public repository as of 05 Aug 2026. The Posts column charts dated items by month over the past 12 months; “–” means no dated post is listed in that range.

                    Papers: the 2023–2026 research index includes papers when the title, abstract, or source metadata contains neurosymbolic, neuro-symbolic, neuro symbolic, neural-symbolic, neural symbolic, or NeSy. Conference metadata comes from the published proceedings source. PDF fallbacks accept only title-and-author validated arXiv versions; when no safe PDF match exists, the index keeps the DOI or source page instead. Institution filters use only affiliations extracted from that paper; missing affiliations are not inferred from author profiles.

                    How the paper dataset is built

                    1. Scope: papers published from 2023 through 2026.
                    2. Discovery: explicit neurosymbolic terms in titles, abstracts, keywords, or source metadata.
                    3. Sources: arXiv, NeSy/DBLP, official IJCAI proceedings, and the pinned ai-conferences/all-papers dataset on Hugging Face for AAAI, ICLR, ICML, and NeurIPS.
                    4. Files: official PDFs are preferred; title-and-author-validated arXiv versions are used only as safe fallbacks.
                    5. Metadata: IJCAI abstracts and DOIs come from official detail pages. Other PDF abstracts and paper-specific affiliations are extracted with GROBID.
                    6. Deduplication: matching records are merged while retaining conference, DOI, source, and resolution provenance.
                    7. Filters: each count updates against the search and every other selected facet; its own facet is excluded so alternative choices remain meaningful.

                    Author and institution filters count every author and every affiliation reported for that specific paper. Missing affiliations are not inferred from current profiles.
