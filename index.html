        Inclusion Method

        NeSy Atlas is a source-backed table of companies whose own homepage, founder, or official announcement explicitly identifies neurosymbolic, neural-symbolic, NeSy, logical-intelligence, or combined neural and symbolic work.

        Primary homepage evidence: the company's root homepage must contain the case-insensitive term neurosymbolic, neuro-symbolic, neural-symbolic, NeSy, or logical intelligence, or explicitly state that it combines neural and symbolic methods. Company names link directly to the qualifying homepage.

        For product-first sites that omit architecture language from the homepage, a direct, attributable statement from a founder or official company announcement may qualify the company. Unattributed third-party descriptions, conference sites, and directories do not qualify a company on their own.

        Current scope: for now, NeSy Atlas focuses on independent and specialist companies and does not include major technology companies, even when they have qualifying neurosymbolic work. This scope may expand in the future.

        Founded years use each company's LinkedIn About page when available. If a founding year cannot be found there, the current CEO's start year is used as the fallback.
        Locations and funding reflect the latest public information found during the 04 Aug 2026 review. Funding links point to the relevant disclosure; "Not disclosed" means no reliable public amount was found. GitHub star totals show each verified organization's most-starred public repository as of 05 Aug 2026. The Posts column charts dated items by month over the past 12 months; “–” means no dated post is listed in that range.
